# Business Model: Independent Executive Support Practice

## Classification

- Repository: `cloud-itonami-isco-3343`
- ISCO-08: `3343`
- Occupation: Administrative and Executive Secretaries
- Social impact: small-business-support, executive-capacity, local-jobs

## Customer

- executives and their offices
- small businesses and sole proprietors who need executive-support capacity

## Offer

- correspondence intake and filing
- calendar and travel scheduling coordination
- authority-concern surfacing (signing-authority / commitment-risk flags)
- office-supply procurement coordination

## Revenue

- monthly retainer
- per-operation fee

## Trust Controls

- no signature or financial/contractual commitment is ever finalized on the
  executive's behalf — that is always the executive's own act, never this
  practice's
- authority-concern flags always require human sign-off, never auto-commit
- no supply order above the executive's registered cost ceiling without the
  governor gate
- correspondence, calendar and supply-order records are auditable, not
  editable
